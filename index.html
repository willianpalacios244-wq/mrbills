What Mr.Bills is

Mr.Bills is a personal content tool used by Stick Rivera to manage his own TikTok account. It lets the account owner sign in with TikTok, upload and publish his own videos, and see basic stats about how his own content performs, all from one place.

How it works

  • The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves access.
  • Videos he has created are uploaded to his TikTok account, either as drafts to review in the TikTok app or published directly with the caption and privacy setting he chooses.
  • Basic profile information and his own follower and like counts are shown so he can track his content.

Who can use it

Mr.Bills is a private tool for one account owner. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other TikTok users.
