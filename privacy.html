1. Who we are

Mr.Bills is a personal content tool operated by Stick Rivera (Stick Rivera Media) for his own TikTok account.

2. What we access

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): display name, avatar and TikTok open ID, used to show which account is connected.
  • Account stats (user.info.stats): follower, following, like and video counts of the connected account, used to track how the content performs.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish): used only to send videos the account owner chooses to his own TikTok account.

3. What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for advertising. We do not collect data about other TikTok users. We do not read your messages.

4. Service providers

The connection to TikTok is handled through Composio, an integration platform, which stores the login token needed to act on the account owner's behalf. Videos and data are sent only between the account owner, Composio and TikTok.

5. How long we keep data

Data is kept only as long as the account stays connected. Disconnecting Mr.Bills in TikTok (Security → Manage app permissions) ends access; you can also ask us to delete any stored data.

6. Security

Access tokens are stored by the integration provider and are never shared publicly.

7. Children

Mr.Bills is not meant for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
